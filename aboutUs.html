About us

Welcome to CS Museum, a virtual museum that celebrates the human mind in two forms: the creativity of art and the logic of computing. Our collections show that these two worlds are closer than they seem. Behind every masterpiece and every machine is a person who looked at the world differently and dared to create something new.



What You'll Find Here

The Pioneers of Computer Science Meet the people who shaped the digital world: Muhammad al-Khwarizmi, Ada Lovelace, Charles Babbage, Alan Turing, Grace Hopper, and Tim Berners-Lee. Their ideas, from the first algorithms to the World Wide Web, power the technology we use every day.


The Masters of Art

Explore the lives and legacies of Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, Vincent van Gogh, Pablo Picasso, Claude Monet, and Frida Kahlo. Their work changed how we see beauty, emotion, and the world around us.

Our Mission
