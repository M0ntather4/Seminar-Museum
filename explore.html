Welcome to our time portal in which we use to see the most influential artists in history


    Leonardo da Vinci (1452-1519)

    An Italian painter, inventor, and scientist, and the ultimate "Renaissance man." He mixed art with anatomy, engineering, and nature.

    Some of his work:

    The last supper

    Painted on a monastery wall in Milan, it captures the exact moment Jesus announces that one of his apostles will betray him, and each face reacts differently.


    Mona Lisa (c. 1503-1519)

    Her mysterious smile seems to change depending on where you look. Leonardo kept the painting with him for years and never delivered it to the client.


    Michelangelo (1475-1564)

    An Italian sculptor, painter, and architect who believed he was freeing figures already trapped inside the marble.

    His work include

    David (1501-1504)

    Carved from a single block of marble that other sculptors had abandoned. Michelangelo was only in his twenties when he finished it.

    Sistine Chapel Ceiling (1508-1512)

    He painted it over about four years, mostly standing and reaching upward, and it features the famous scene of God reaching out to Adam.

    Pietà (1498-1499)

    Mary holding the body of Jesus. It is the only work Michelangelo ever signed, with his name carved on the sash across her chest.


    Vincent van Gogh (1853-1890)

    A Dutch painter known for bold colors and swirling brushstrokes. He sold very few paintings in his lifetime and became world famous only after his death.

    Some of his most famous work include:

    The Starry Night (1889)

    Painted from memory of the view outside his window while he was staying at an asylum in Saint-Rémy, France.

    Sunflowers (1888)

    A series he painted to decorate the room of his friend, the artist Paul Gauguin, who was coming to stay with him.

    Bedroom in Arles

    His small, simple room painted in bright colors. He loved it so much that he painted it three times.
